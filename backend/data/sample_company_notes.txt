Company Overview

Our company sells subscription software to small businesses. We track
customer churn every quarter and review it with the leadership team.

Q1 Churn Notes
Churn in Q1 was driven mainly by price sensitivity among customers on the
Starter plan. Several customers mentioned that competitor products offered
a lower entry price with similar core features.

Q2 Churn Notes
Churn dropped in Q2 after we introduced an annual billing discount. Support
response time also improved, which several exit surveys cited as a reason
customers considered staying but ultimately left for other reasons, such as
a change in their own business needs.

Q3 Churn Notes
Q3 churn increased again, concentrated in the Enterprise tier. Feedback
pointed to missing integrations with popular accounting tools as the
primary driver. Sales flagged this as a recurring objection during renewal
conversations.

Product Roadmap Notes
The engineering team is prioritizing accounting-tool integrations for the
next release cycle, directly in response to the Q3 churn feedback.
